# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + CSS + Vanilla JavaScript. Single-page application with scrollspy navigation.

## Users

Primary: Local families, couples, corporate clients, young urban professionals (25-40), and tourists seeking fine dining. All share one goal: explore the restaurant and book a table.

## Product Purpose

RoyalBite is a premium restaurant website that showcases its identity, displays the menu, and drives table reservation bookings. Success = visitor leaves with a confirmed reservation or strong intent to visit.

## Positioning

Premium Indian fine-dining blending authentic regional flavours with contemporary presentation, 20+ years of culinary excellence, and a warm welcoming atmosphere.

## Operating Context

- Desktop and mobile browsers.
- Single-page app; all sections (Home, About, Menu, Reservation, Gallery, Contact) via sticky scrollspy nav.
- Reservation form is the primary conversion action.
- No online ordering.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site, no backend. Forms use JS toast feedback.
- Menu rendered dynamically from JS data array.
- Font stack: Cormorant Garamond (serif) + Plus Jakarta Sans (body).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: RoyalBite Fine Dine Restaurant
- Palette: Cream #FAF7F2, Olive Green #1E392A, Amber #E58B24
- Feel: Warm, premium, welcoming
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond headings, Plus Jakarta Sans body

## Evidence on Hand

- Working codebase: index.html, styles.css, app.js
- Unsplash placeholder images; no real photography yet

## Product Principles

1. Reservation-first: guide every visitor toward booking a table.
2. Premium without pretension: elegant yet warm.
3. Simplicity over features: focused and fast.
4. Authentic identity: palette and typography are brand anchors.
5. Mobile-ready: full experience on phone screens.
